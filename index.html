Experimental Condensed Matter Physics

Srishti Dixit

I am a researcher in experimental condensed matter physics with experience in magnetic and quantum materials, including Heusler alloys, topological systems, thin films, and bulk and single-crystal samples.

My work focuses on synthesis, characterization, magnetic and magneto-transport measurements, and analysis of materials using techniques such as XRD, MPMS, PLD, low-temperature cryostats, Raman spectroscopy, XAS/XMCD, XPS, and SEM.

CV Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn

Research

My Ph.D. research at the Department of Physics, Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi examined the magnetic and magneto-transport properties of Heusler alloys. I have worked on bulk systems, single crystals, and thin films, combining experimental synthesis with structural, magnetic, transport, thermal, and spectroscopic analysis.

Research Interests

  • Magnetic and quantum materials
  • Heusler alloys and frustrated magnetic systems
  • Magnetoresistance, anomalous Hall effect, and spin-valve behavior
  • Topological insulators and heterostructures
  • Thin-film growth and low-temperature measurements

Methods and Tools

  • Arc melting, solid-state reaction, and modified Bridgman crystal growth
  • Pulse laser deposition, XRD, MPMS, PPMS, and cryostat-based measurements
  • Raman spectroscopy, XAS/XMCD, XPS, SEM, and transport measurements
  • Python, Quantum ESPRESSO, HighScore Plus, LabVIEW, Gnuplot, Origin, and Xmgrace
